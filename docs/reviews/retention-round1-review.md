# Retention, round 1 review: the plan - 5.9 / 10 (getting better)

One independent Claude reviewer (brief: `retention-round1.md`). Order 6.5, completeness 6.0, risk
5.0, economy 5.5, buildability 5.5.

## Claims checked
- Levels 16-50 pay nothing: true, and so do levels 2, 4, 7, 10 and 13. "Every second match" is
  wrong past the early levels: level 49 needs 860 XP, about 6 matches; 16-50 is about 160 matches.
- The farming limit (45 s, a hit, Training excluded, 12 an hour, CPU 0.75): true.
- Only first-win and the featured shop key on the day: true.
- The brief's craft-cost range was wrong: 30-8000 (Mythic 8000).

## What would raise the score, ranked
1. Resolve the hourly cap against the session goals: 12 matches is about 36 minutes; after it
   everything the plan adds stops moving, unsaid.
2. Real numbers for steps 1, 2 and 4; weekly keys cut to one (four-plus earned keys a week is over
   300 R$ of value and undercuts key sales and the Season Pass).
3. PLAY AGAIN and the near-miss first (cheapest, no economy risk, reaches new players); the friend
   invite prompt early.
4. First session, low population and solo play, lapsed players.
5. Anti-abuse in the steps: CPU quest weighting, friend bonus alt checks, no PARTY in Ranked, a
   streak day needs a counted match, no keys on the streak.
6. Settle back-pay and the title system.
7. Analytics events before step 1, for a baseline.

dailies 50 XP and 20-30 Sparks each; weeklies 60-100 Sparks each and one key for all three; streak
20/25/30/35/40/50 Sparks and a Scrap Crate on day 7.
