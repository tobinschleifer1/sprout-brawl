Weapon animation sheet

Every attack in the game, one filmstrip per move, drawn by the renderer the match uses — body, weapon and all, through Renderer2D.drawFighterInto. Frames run left to right across startup, active (marked with a gold bar) and recovery.

This page used to paint its own grey rectangles for the body and its own hand-rolled transform for the weapon, so it agreed with the game on nothing and the animation was being reviewed from a picture the game never drew. A tool that does not share the renderer lies eventually.
